# Public summary

I built an interpretable content-refresh review queue on FlyRank's pseudonymized snapshot. A nested client-grouped audit measured Decision Tree Precision@50 of 0.540 versus 0.280 for the transparent baseline. That is retrospective ranking of a current-decline proxy—not proof that refreshing pages improves traffic. Read the paper and reproducible notebooks: https://github.com/sinagdevv/flyrank-ml-internship-sinagdev
